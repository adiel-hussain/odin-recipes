Kashmiri chicken(murgh)

Description

kashmiri murgh is a traditional chickenn dish prepared with chicken, yogurt, kashmiri spices, and aromatic whole spices . It has a rich, flavourful gravy and is usually served with rice.

Ingredients

  • chicken
  • yogurt
  • mustard oil
  • kashmiri red chilli powder
  • fennel powder
  • ginger powder
  • cumin seeds

Steps

  1. Heat oil and add the whole spices.
  2. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned
  3. Add yogurt and spices, stirring well
  4. Add water and salt , then cover and cook until the chicken is tender
  5. simmer the gravy for a few minutes and serve not with rice
