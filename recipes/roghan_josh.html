Roghan josh

Description

it is a famous traditional kashmiri mutton dish. it is made with tender pieces of mutton cooked slowly in a rich, aromatic gravy using kashmiri spices. the dish is known for its deep red colour, favourful taste and fragrant aroma. it is commonly served with steamed rice or kashmiri pulao.

Ingredients

  • mutton - (500g)
  • mustard oil - (4tbsp)
  • yogurt - (150g)
  • kashmiri red chilli powder - (2tsp)
  • fennel powder - (2tsp)
  • ginger powder - (1tsp)
  • cumin seeds - (1/2 tsp)
  • bay leaf - (1)
  • cardamon - (1)
  • cloves - (4)

steps

  1. Heat oil and add whole spices like cardamon,cloves,and bay leaf.
  2. Add mutton and cook until it becomes lightly browned
  3. Add yogurt and spices - kashmiri chilli , fennel powder , ginger powder, and hing
  4. Add water and salt, cover and cook on low heat until the mutton becomes tender
  5. sinmmer the gravy for a few more minutes , then serve hot with rice
