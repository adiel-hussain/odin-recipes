Yakhni

Description

yakhni is a traditional kashmiri mutton dish made with mutton, yogurt, and aromatic whole spices. it has a mild, creamy gravy and a delicate fragrance

ingredients

  • mutton - (500g)
  • yogurt - (200g)
  • mustard oil - (3tbsp)
  • fennel powder - (2tsp)
  • ginger powder - (1tsp)
  • cardamon -(4)

Steps

  1. heat oil and add cumin, cardoman,cloves and bay leaf
  2. Add mutton and cook until lightly browned
  3. add water and salt, cover, and cook until the mutton becomes tender
  4. Whisk the yogurt with fennel and ginger powder, then slowly add it to the cooked mutton while stirring
  5. simmer on low heat for 10-15 minutes until the gravy becomes smooth and aromatic
